ONE VERB: FLIP
GAME IN 10 WORDS: Enemies orbit you — flip their direction to chain collisions.
WHAT THE PLAYER MASTERS: Reading multi-ring orbital phases and timing the flip to align enemy clusters at the same collision point
THE WOW MOMENT: On wave 5, a second enemy ring enters already orbiting in the opposite direction — a single flip sends both rings through each other simultaneously, wiping 12 enemies in a chain explosion the player didn't plan but instantly understood
WHY RICARDO: R-Type — you read the formation before it reaches kill range, anticipation is the entire loop; Tony Hawk — chain kills compound into multipliers and a perfectly timed flip feels exactly like landing a trick combo you called three seconds early
EMOTIONAL ARC:
  start: Three enemies circle you on a visible orbit arc, like a slow clock hand; a tap button pulses once; player taps out of curiosity; enemies reverse; two collide; explode; brain fires — no text needed
  groove: Fluency feels like conducting — you stop tracking individual enemies and start reading gaps, timing the flip so 4–5 enemies pile into the same collision point like a closing fist
  climax: Three concentric rings at different speeds; one flip remaining before cooldown resets; you're projecting three orbital phases simultaneously trying to find the single angle where all leading clusters align
WHAT MAKES IT HARD: Enemies orbit at different speeds per ring — gaps open and close in seconds; you must project future positions, not react to current ones; flip cooldown means a wasted flip is a death sentence
SKILL CEILING: 1x player flips when enemies happen to be near each other. 10x player mentally tracks all three ring phases, identifies the one moment every 8 seconds where every ring's leading cluster converges at the same angle, flips once, chains the entire wave in a single detonation
VISUAL: #0A0A1A (void black), #FF4D00 (orbital fire), #00E5FF (player core glow) — top-down locked camera, aesthetic: Osmos × Wipeout HUD geometry
AUDIO: 128 BPM, pulsing sub-bass throb locked to each orbit revolution, signature SFX: deep metallic CLANG-cascade that rings out differently for each enemy added to a chain
NOT: Avoids twin-stick shooter (no aiming at all); avoids Space Invaders formation (enemies never move toward you — they only orbit)
MECHANIC SELF-EVIDENT CHECK: Player sees a glowing core (themselves) at center. Three enemies travel along a clearly drawn curved orbit line, visibly looping like clock hands. A single tap button pulses. Player taps — enemies immediately reverse, two collide, explode. Cause and effect lands in under 2 seconds. Zero text required.
